9. GAPS TO CLOSE BEFORE YOU CAN SELL SUBSCRIPTIONS
===============================================================
These are code issues, not assets — but they block launch.
Found while checking the existing Stripe setup.

(a) PLAN IDS DO NOT MATCH ACROSS THE CODEBASE
    SignupPage.jsx offers:  starter | pro      | business
    config.js defines:      starter | professional | enterprise
    Stripe price IDs:       starter | professional | enterprise
    -> Choosing "Pro" at signup looks up priceIds['pro'], which is
       undefined, and checkout fails with "Invalid plan".
       Pick one naming scheme and use it in all three places.

(b) THE POST-CHECKOUT PAGE DOES NOT EXIST
    stripe.js redirects to /settings/billing?success=1 and
    ?cancelled=1, but Settings has no "billing" route — the tabs are
    company, branding, stamp, team, numbering, profile.
    -> Build a Billing tab: current plan, usage vs limits, invoices,
       payment method, upgrade/downgrade, cancel.

(c) PLAN LIMITS ARE DEFINED BUT NEVER ENFORCED
    config.js sets maxClients / maxInvoices / maxUsers per plan, but
    nothing checks them when creating clients, invoices or users.
    -> Enforce, or the paid tiers have no functional difference.

(d) NO PRICING SHOWN ANYWHERE BUT SIGNUP
    Prices ($19 / $49) are hardcoded in SignupPage.jsx only. The
    marketing site will need the same numbers — put them in one
    shared source so they cannot drift.

(e) NO ANNUAL BILLING OPTION
    Only monthly price IDs exist. If you want the "save 20% annually"
    badge in section 5a, you need annual price IDs too.
