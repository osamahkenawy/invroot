codebase, not generic advice — the source of each number is noted.

===============================================================
PART B — TENANT-UPLOADED ASSETS
===============================================================
These are what each customer uploads in Settings. Sizes are derived
from where they actually render, at 3x for print-quality PDF output.
Limits are enforced in src/middleware/upload.js.

--- B1. Company logo ---
Uploaded at:  Settings > Company Profile, or inline from the invoice form
Renders at:   36px tall  (app sidebar — Layout.css .brand-logo)
              120 x 90px (invoice form logo box — InvoiceFormModal.css)
              60px tall  (invoice + receipt PDF — pdf.js .company-logo img)

RECOMMENDED UPLOAD      600 x 200 px   (3:1, transparent PNG)
MINIMUM                 300 x 100 px
MAXIMUM FILE SIZE       5 MB           (hard limit, enforced)
ACCEPTED                PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF
NOTES                   Transparent PNG strongly preferred — the logo
                        sits on navy in the sidebar and on white in the
                        PDF, so a baked-in background will look wrong in
                        one of the two. Wide/horizontal art fits the
                        120x90 box better than a square.

--- B2. Official stamp ---
Uploaded at:  Settings > Stamp & Signature
Renders at:   160 x 90px max (invoice PDF — pdf.js .stamp-box img)
              140 x 80px max (receipt PDF)

RECOMMENDED UPLOAD      480 x 270 px   (16:9, transparent PNG)
MINIMUM                 320 x 180 px
MAXIMUM FILE SIZE       2 MB           (hard limit, enforced)
ACCEPTED                PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF
NOTES                   Must be a transparent PNG — it is rendered at
                        92% opacity over the invoice, so a white box
                        will cover the content behind it.

--- B3. Authorised signature ---
Uploaded at:  Settings > Stamp & Signature
Renders at:   same box as the stamp (160 x 90px max)

RECOMMENDED UPLOAD      480 x 270 px   (16:9, transparent PNG)
MINIMUM                 320 x 180 px
MAXIMUM FILE SIZE       2 MB           (hard limit, enforced)
ACCEPTED                PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF
NOTES                   Transparent PNG required, same reason as above.
                        Dark ink on transparent scans best.

--- B4. Generic uploads (attachments / misc images) ---
MAXIMUM FILE SIZE       10 MB          (MAX_FILE_SIZE_MB in .env)
ACCEPTED (images)       PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF
ACCEPTED (documents)    the above + PDF

===============================================================
GENERATION PROMPTS FOR THE 4 MASTERS
===============================================================

--- MASTER 1 — Icon-only mark (1024 x 1024, transparent) ---
CONCEPT: an abstract "root / upward growth" mark, not a document icon.
Every invoicing app uses a page/sheet icon; this trades that cliche for
one bold geometric symbol built around the word ROOT itself.

"A bold, minimal abstract logomark for a premium invoicing SaaS brand
called INVROOT. Do NOT use a document, page, invoice sheet, folded
corner, or checkmark - avoid any literal paper/invoice imagery
entirely. Instead: a single clean geometric symbol made of 2-3 straight
or gently curved lines branching upward from one point, like a
simplified root or growth mark - evoking 'root' as foundation and
upward growth. The mark must be bold, solid, single-color, radically
simple - the level of reduction and confidence of a Nike swoosh or an
airline tail-fin mark, not an illustration. Rendered as one solid shape
in bright gold (#D4AF37), centered on a solid deep navy (#0D1B2A)
rounded-square (squircle) background, generous padding on all sides.
Flat vector style, no gradients, no shadows, no outline-only strokes -
must read as a bold, instantly recognizable mark even shrunk to 16px.
Professional, premium, financial/fintech feel, confident and simple,
zero clutter."

--- MASTER 2 — Lockup for light backgrounds (2400 x 600, transparent) ---
"A horizontal logo lockup for a premium B2B invoicing SaaS brand called
INVROOT. On the left, a small bold abstract mark: 2-3 clean lines
branching upward from a single point, like a simplified root/growth
symbol - NOT a document or page icon, no literal paper imagery -
filled as one solid shape in deep navy (#0D1B2A). To the right of the
mark, the wordmark 'INVROOT' in a bold, modern, geometric sans-serif
(like Inter Bold or Space Grotesk Bold), all capital letters, solid
deep navy (#0D1B2A), tight letter-spacing. Every letter must be fully
solid, ordinary type - no hollow letters, no circles or holes cut into
any letter, no decorative treatment on individual characters.
Optionally add ONE small gold (#8A6D1F) accent only - for example a
short underline beneath the wordmark - and nothing more. Flat vector
design, transparent background, generous whitespace, no shadows, no
gradients. Confident, premium, minimal fintech aesthetic."

--- MASTER 3 — Lockup for dark backgrounds (2400 x 600, transparent) ---
"Same horizontal lockup as above - the abstract branching root/growth
mark on the left (2-3 clean lines from one point, NOT a document or
page icon), the 'INVROOT' wordmark in bold geometric sans-serif on the
right - but recolored for a dark navy background: the mark is solid
bright gold (#D4AF37), and the wordmark 'INVROOT' is solid white.
Every letter must be fully solid ordinary type - no hollow letters, no
circles or holes cut into any letter. Transparent background so it sits
cleanly on a deep navy (#0D1B2A) surface. Same flat vector style, no
shadows, no gradients."

--- MASTER 4 — Social share image (1200 x 630) ---
"A wide social-share preview image (1200x630) for a billing/invoicing
SaaS product called INVROOT. Deep navy background (#0D1B2A) with a
subtle darker navy (#060E15) radial gradient glow in one corner. On the
left third, the INVROOT logo lockup in white with a bright gold
(#D4AF37) accent - the abstract branching root mark plus the wordmark.
On the right two-thirds, a clean abstract illustration suggesting
invoices/documents: 2-3 overlapping simplified invoice cards at a
slight angle, drawn as minimal white and gold outlines, no readable
text on the cards. Below or beside the logo, a short tagline in white,
smaller weight: 'Billing, Simplified.' Premium fintech aesthetic, lots
of negative space, flat design, no photos, no 3D, no gradients on the
invoice cards themselves - only the background may carry a subtle
gradient."
